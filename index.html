Our approach

AI workflows need more than automation.

NAOF is being developed around explicit constraints and verifiable evidence, rather than treating agent outputs as automatically trustworthy.

01 / CONTROL

Deterministic orchestration

Structured workflow states, predefined decision boundaries, and clearly scoped execution paths.

02 / EVIDENCE

Traceability first

Records and checkpoints designed to make workflow decisions and outcomes inspectable.

03 / OVERSIGHT

Human authority

Separating automated processing, independent review, and decisions that require explicit human approval.

Development status

Research-led. Tested. Still evolving.

NAOF is an early-stage engineering project, not a production-ready commercial service. The reference implementation has a deterministic, local read-only foundation; further workflow capabilities are being developed and independently evaluated.

  • Documented architecture and implementation boundaries
  • Read-only kernel and routing foundation
  • Automated testing and independent audit practices
  • Ongoing development of controlled shadow workflows
Current position
Pre-commercial development

Product-market fit, integrations and production deployment remain future work. No live customer deployment or general-purpose autonomous agent runtime is claimed.

Long-term vision

AI systems organizations can understand and govern.

Our goal is to explore practical ways for teams to coordinate AI-assisted operations with clear responsibility, evidence and review. Potential application areas include software delivery, research workflows and accountable business automation.

These are directions under exploration, not capabilities or commercial offerings currently available.

What guides the work

Verifiable outcomes over opaque claims.

How development proceeds

Bounded scope, tests, documented limitations and iterative evaluation.

What comes next

Technical validation, product discovery and future collaboration opportunities.
